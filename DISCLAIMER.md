# Disclaimer

This site is a free, independent compilation of public records and published news about large data-center and
industrial projects in northeast Oklahoma, made for public information. It is not for sale.

## Accuracy

- Every item links to its source. Confirm anything important with the agency or company that published the record
  before relying on it.
- **Facts only.** Every item is a public record, an official or company statement, a cited news report, or a dated
  field observation. Nothing is inferred: where no record connects two things (for example, a company and a permit, or
  two companies), the site does not connect them.
- Summaries condense the cited sources; they are not complete copies of them.
- Map positions are approximate and not survey-grade. Parcel outlines come from county parcel maps where those are
  published; elsewhere they are drawn from recorded legal descriptions on the federal survey grid and are marked as
  approximate. Permit points are the location given by the filer, not necessarily the work site.
- Entries marked **from records** are generated automatically from the map's data (permit filings, recorded deeds,
  City Council easements) and carry the same caveats.
- Records change. The date each dataset was read is shown with it; later filings may supersede what is shown here.
