# Oklahoma Data Center Map

An interactive map and timeline of large data-center and industrial projects across Oklahoma, including
Meta's Project Anthem in east Tulsa and the surrounding Fair Oaks MPD-6 development, Google's Pryor and
Clydesdale sites, Projects Mustang and Spring, the Port of Inola smelter, Core Scientific and sites in Oklahoma City and elsewhere. It shows zoning,
land records, site plans, permits, easements and power infrastructure. Every item links to its public source.

Built with [Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com/) on Esri World Imagery. The site is in `docs/`.
