About this site

A free, independent compilation of public records and published news about large data-center and industrial projects in northeast Oklahoma, for public information. Not for sale.

Accuracy

  • Every item links to its source. Confirm anything important with the agency or company that published the record before relying on it.
  • Facts only. Every item is a public record, an official or company statement, a cited news report, or a dated field observation. Nothing is inferred: where no record connects two things, the site does not connect them.
  • Map positions are approximate and not survey-grade. Outlines come from county parcel maps where published; elsewhere they are drawn from recorded legal descriptions and marked approximate. Permit points are the filer’s location, not necessarily the work site.
  • Timeline entries marked from records are generated automatically from the map’s data and carry the same caveats. Records change; later filings may supersede what is shown.
